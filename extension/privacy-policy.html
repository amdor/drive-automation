Privacy policy

Privacy Policy

Drive Batch Organizer is designed to help you select local photos and videos, organize them, and upload them to your Google Drive account. We respect your privacy and we keep the app minimal and local-first.

Information we access

  • Google Drive account access only when you choose to connect the extension.
  • Your selected local files and folders only while you pick a directory and run the upload.
  • Metadata needed for the upload workflow, such as file names, sizes, creation dates, and resumable upload progress.

How we use the information

The extension uses Google OAuth and the Google Drive API to create folders in your Drive account and upload selected media files. It does not use your data for advertising, analytics, profiling, or resale. The app stores queue state locally in your browser profile so a transfer can resume.

Local storage

The extension stores session and queue information in the browser's local IndexedDB storage on your device. This is used for resumable uploads and retry flows. It does not send that metadata to any third-party service except the Google Drive API when you actively upload files.

Google account data

When you connect Drive, Chrome's identity API obtains an OAuth access token for the Drive API. That token is used only to create folders and upload files. We do not collect or sell your Google account data.

Changes

This policy may be updated if the app's functionality changes. Any material changes will be posted in this page and in the extension update notes.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact the extension publisher through the Chrome Web Store listing or the support contact provided for the published extension.
